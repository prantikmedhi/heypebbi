02 / Familiar tools, a world of character

Expressive outside.
Clear where it counts.

A living-desk composition gives the app its personality. Native navigation, opaque reading planes and truthful state labels do the work. Both studies below are noninteractive design references.

04 / The character stays. The state changes.

A face with
good manners.

A high shoulder. A broad base. A tiny clay thumbprint. One original silhouette, with clear expressions that support the text—never replace it.

Static expression studies. Listening requires a genuinely live voice session; approval, failure and task progress keep their own labeled controls. Reduced motion keeps every state readable and every idle Pebbi still.
